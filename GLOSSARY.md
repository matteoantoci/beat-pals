# Beat Pals

Beat Pals is a private rhythm game for one child of 4 to 5 years. Notes fall to three pals in time with a real song, and the child taps the lane of a pal on the beat.

## People

**Child**:
The one player of the game, 4 to 5 years old, who cannot read.
_Avoid_: User, player, kid

**Parent**:
The adult who prepares the songs and uses the parent area.
_Avoid_: Admin, grown-up, guardian

## The band

**Pal**:
One of the three jelly characters: Pip (left), Dot (middle), and Bo (right).
_Avoid_: Character, avatar, monster, buddy

**Band**:
The pals that have a lane in the current play.
_Avoid_: Team, group

**Waiting pal**:
A pal that is not in the band yet and stands at the side of the band.
_Avoid_: Locked pal, inactive pal

**Lane**:
The column of one pal, from the top of the screen to its ring, where its notes fall and where a tap counts for it.
_Avoid_: Track, column, fret, string

**Ring**:
The target at the bottom of a lane, above its pal, where a note must be tapped.
_Avoid_: Target, hit zone, strike line

## Songs and charts

**Song**:
One piece of recorded music with its chart and its picture.
_Avoid_: Track, level, tune

**Chart**:
The list of notes for a song, made by a person for Clone Hero or Guitar Hero.
_Avoid_: Beatmap, map, note track, human chart

**Fret**:
One of the five guitar buttons of a chart (or the open note), before the game maps it to a lane.
_Avoid_: Button, color, key

**Chord**:
Two or more frets at the same time in a chart.
_Avoid_: Multi-note, combo note

**Child chart**:
The simpler list of notes that the game makes from a chart for one play, from the record color and the speed step.
_Avoid_: Easy chart, filtered chart, difficulty

**Family key**:
The secret code that opens the songs of the family; the parent enters it one time on the device.
_Avoid_: Password, token, license key, song key

**Song setup**:
The guided steps in the parent area that install the app, take the family key, and download the songs.
_Avoid_: Import, sync, onboarding

## Play

**Play**:
One time through one song, from its start to the end scene.
_Avoid_: Round, game, run, attempt, session

**Note**:
One thing in the child chart that the child must tap in a lane at one time.
_Avoid_: Gem, candy, bubble, beat

**Soft hold**:
A note that also asks the child to keep the finger down; it is done at half of its length, and an early release is never a miss.
_Avoid_: Sustain, long note, hold note

**Double**:
A note on two lanes next to each other that counts when either lane is tapped.
_Avoid_: Chord, two-lane note, multi-button note

**Hit**:
A tap that matches a note in time; a great hit is closer to the note time than a good hit.
_Avoid_: Score, perfect, correct

**Miss**:
A note that passes with no hit; it changes nothing.
_Avoid_: Error, fail, mistake

**Band energy**:
The mood of the band in a play, which rises with hits through steps 1, 2, 3, and gold, and falls only one step at a time.
_Avoid_: Combo, streak, multiplier, power, hype

**Level-up moment**:
The short sound and effect when band energy reaches a new step.
_Avoid_: Bonus, achievement

**Phrase**:
A short run of notes for a star moment: a Star Power phrase of the human chart, or a phrase that a rule makes when the chart has none.
_Avoid_: Star Power, section, combo

**Phrase ball**:
A note of a phrase. It falls as the usual ball with a wide gold glow and a thick double gold ring.
_Avoid_: Star note, gold note, special note

**Star moment**:
The reward when the child hits at least 3 in 4 notes of a phrase: speed lines and sparkles at the screen edges, all three pals cheer, and the star moment sound plays. A missed phrase costs nothing.
_Avoid_: Star Power, bonus, combo, gold move

## Progress

**Record color**:
The level of skill on one song. There are 5 levels: level 0, bronze, silver, gold, and platinum. Each level above level 0 adds one new skill. Level 0 shows no record. The first good play at level 0 earns the bronze record.
_Avoid_: Level, cup, medal, trophy, rank, tier

**Star**:
A mark of progress inside a record color, earned by one play with a score of 80% or more; a star is never lost.
_Avoid_: Point, reward

**Score**:
The result of a play: the notes hit divided by the notes plus the empty taps. The child never sees it as a number.
_Avoid_: Points, hit ratio, accuracy

**Empty tap**:
A tap that hits no note and lowers the score. At level 0, it is a second tap on a hit note or a tap in a rest. At the colors (bronze and up), it is a tap in another band lane, inside the good window of a hit note. It changes nothing in the play. After two weak plays at level 0, no tap is empty there.
_Avoid_: Wrong tap, false tap, mistake, overstrum

**Speed step**:
The beat pattern of a play, set by the stars of the current record color: beats 1 and 3 of each bar, then 3 of 4 beats, then every beat.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, tempo, speed level

**Weak play**:
A play with a score below 80%.
_Avoid_: Failed play, lost play, bad play

**Step down**:
The help after two weak plays in a row: the next play uses the speed step one below, inside the same record color.
_Avoid_: Easy mode, penalty, demotion

## Screens

**Title screen**:
The first screen, with the logo, the wordmark, the pals on the stage floor, and the title music. It shows the play button only when a song is ready.
_Avoid_: Home screen, splash, start screen

**Song list**:
The screen with one song card for each song that is ready to play.
_Avoid_: Library, catalog, menu, playlist

**Song card**:
The picture of a song in the song list, with its record and its stars.
_Avoid_: Tile, item, cover

**Load scene**:
The short scene while a song loads: its cover on a turning record, with a fill ring, and the pals in the ready state. When the load fails, the pals make the oops face.
_Avoid_: Spinner, loading screen, progress bar

**End scene**:
The short reward screen after a play.
_Avoid_: Results screen, score screen, game over

**Join scene**:
The scene where a waiting pal jumps into the band, at bronze and silver.
_Avoid_: Unlock scene

**Parent check**:
The multiplication question that a parent answers after a 3 s hold on the logo.
_Avoid_: Parental gate, lock, PIN

**Parent area**:
The adult screen with the song setup, the sound switch, the save code, the reset, and the credits.
_Avoid_: Settings, options, admin panel

**Save code**:
A text that holds all progress, so that the parent can copy it and restore it.
_Avoid_: Backup, export, password

## Sessions

**Session**:
The plays between a wake-up and the goodbye. It has a time limit in minutes.
_Avoid_: Concert, round, game

**Sun stage**:
The stage of the sun in a session: day, late afternoon, or sunset.
_Avoid_: Level, phase

**Last song**:
The song that starts when the song list shows the sunset, or after the time limit is used.
_Avoid_: Final level

**Last-song cue**:
The full-screen sunset on the load scene of the last song, with its tune.

**Goodbye scene**:
The scene after the last song, from the wave to the sleep.

**Night title**:
The title screen while the pals sleep, with no play button.

**Rest time**:
The time from the goodbye to the next session. The game calculates it from the settings.
_Avoid_: Rest, break, cooldown ("Rest" is already a gap in the notes.)

**Session spacing**:
The shortest time from the start of one session to the start of the next: 12 h divided by the sessions per day.

**Wake-up scene**:
The scene where the moon goes down, the sun comes up, and the pals wake.
